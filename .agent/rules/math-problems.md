# Math problems

- Generate problems in `problems.js`. Keep `app.js` as UI and round state.
- `pictures` is a hardness setting that only generates easy, countable problems (add/sub up to 8, multiplication factors 2–9) and renders kid-friendly drawings of fruit, toys, and school supplies (pens, pencils, erasers, crayons) to count instead of the column/long worksheets. Pictures never includes division: `playOps` drops `div`, the ÷ chip is disabled, and `generateProblem` will not pick it.
- Easy is a flat equation with one blank for the whole answer: `7 + 4 = __`, `8 − 3 = _`, `5 × 3 = __`, `15 ÷ 3 = _`. The blank is as wide as the answer. Do not stack easy problems in columns or long division.
- Medium and hard addition is vertical column addition: addends stacked, `+` on the left of the lower number, a rule, then the total. Fill one digit at a time from right to left. When a column sums to 10 or more, its carry is a fillable box above the next column, written before the next digit.
- Medium and hard subtraction is stacked the same way, with `−` on the left of the lower number. Fill from right to left. When a column must borrow, that borrow is a fillable carry box on that column, then the answer digit.
- Multiplication at medium and hard is long multiplication: stacked factors, a rule, partial products when the multiplier has more than one digit, then the total. The larger factor is on top. Easy multiplication is `[2–9] × [2–9]` as a flat equation. Medium is a 2–3 digit factor × 3–9. Hard is a 3–5 digit factor × a 2–3 digit factor. Do not show a redundant `a × b =` line above a stack. On the stack, digits are filled one at a time from right to left: ones digit, then the tens carry, then the tens digit, and so on. Carry cells are fillable circled digits, two type sizes smaller than the working digits, in a cursive face, stacked above the top factor. The first step’s carries sit immediately above the top number; each later step adds a new carry row above the previous one. Each partial row is the full product of the top number times that bottom digit — add the previous carry into the next place (`26 × 2` is `52`: write `2`, carry `1`, tens `5`; `17 × 9` is `153`: write `3`, carry `6`, tens `5`, hundreds `1`; `324 × 7` is `2268`). A leftover after the last top digit is the next result digit (`9 × 3` is `27`). `24 × 8` is `192`. Shifted partials skip the placeholder zeros: `9 × 1` is only `9` moved one column left, not `90`; `11 × 2` is `22` shifted, not `220`. A one-digit multiplier fills the product digits and carries only, including a leading leftover digit. A two-or-more-digit multiplier requires every partial and its carries, then the total digits and any summation carries.
- Division at medium and hard is long division: the bracket, quotient, and subtract-and-bring-down working. Easy division is a flat equation: a dividend from 10–99 divided by 2–9, exact, never `X ÷ X`, and never a dividend made only of the divisor digit (`22 ÷ 2`, `33 ÷ 3`, … `99 ÷ 9`). Medium is a 3–5 digit dividend ÷ 3–9. Hard is a 4–7 digit dividend ÷ a two-digit divisor. Do not show a divisor times table. Every required box on a long-division sheet is a single digit: each quotient digit, each multiply (divisor × quotient digit) digit, each subtract remainder digit, and each bring-down. The final quotient alone is not enough. Worksheet cells must show `0` (never treat it as empty), so `220 ÷ 20` stays `220`, not `22`. `220 ÷ 20` is `11`. Division stays exact; do not generate a remainder problem without a remainder field. A miss on a worksheet does not rewrite the problem as inline text (`Not quite. 220 ÷ 20 = 11`); reveal the filled sheet on a one-try miss, and in practice say try this step again.
- Pictures and easy stay one blank. Medium and hard addition and subtraction fill every answer digit from right to left, plus a carry or borrow box whenever that column needs one. Every carry field, including multiplication carries, is two type sizes smaller than a working digit and uses a cursive face.
- `worksheetFields(problem)` lists every slot the learner must fill. Multi-step multiplication and division are grouped by `worksheetSections`. The button is **Next** until the last section, then **Submit**. Next only advances when the current section is filled and correct. Division shows one quotient digit at a time, with that step’s multiply / subtract / bring-down. The keypad writes the active slot; tapping a slot selects it. Cell slots take one digit and then move to the next slot in the same section. The separate answer box stays hidden.
- Division must be exact. Subtraction must not produce a negative answer. Never generate 0 or 1 as an addend, subtrahend, factor, or divisor (`4 + 1`, `9 − 0`, `6 × 1`, `8 ÷ 1` are invalid). Subtraction is never `X − X`. Division is never `X ÷ X`.
- `parseAnswer` accepts integers only, including a leading unicode minus (`−`).
- The play answer field is keypad-only: `readonly`, no system keyboard, and no typing into the field.
- Stay on the problem until the answer is correct, then advance. A miss scores 0 for that problem even if they later get it right (`creditsAnswer`). Do not jump to the next question on a miss. Results Correct is `formatCorrectCount(correct, answered)` — first-try correct over submitted answers (`10 / 11`).
- Quiz progress stars use `progressStars(correct, limit)`: one star animates in for each 20% of the quiz answered correctly, up to five.
- Unlimited practice and sprint use `unlimitedStars(attempts)`: one star for each all-correct set of 10 problems, counted from the first problem. A retried miss is not totally correct. After 7 stars, show `X ★` instead of more icons.
- Do not skip uniqueness forever; avoiding the immediate previous prompt is enough.
- After changing generation, run `node --experimental-default-type=module test-problems.mjs`.
