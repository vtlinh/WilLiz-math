# Math problems

- Generate problems in `problems.js`. Keep `app.js` as UI and round state.
- `pictures` is a hardness setting that only generates easy, countable problems (add/sub up to 8, mul factors up to 4) and renders kid-friendly drawings of fruit, toys, and school supplies (pens, pencils, erasers, crayons) to count instead of the column/long worksheets. Pictures never includes division: `playOps` drops `div`, the ÷ chip is disabled, and `generateProblem` will not pick it.
- Addition is shown as vertical column addition: addends stacked, `+` on the left of the lower number, a rule, then the total. Do not show `a + b` as a single inline equation only.
- Subtraction is shown the same way, stacked with `−` on the left of the lower number. Do not show `a − b` as a single inline equation only.
- Multiplication is shown as long multiplication: stacked factors, a rule, partial products when the multiplier has more than one digit, then the total. The larger factor is on top. Hard is a 2–3 digit factor × 3–9. Challenge is a 3–5 digit factor × a 2–3 digit factor. Do not show a redundant `a × b =` line above the stack. Digits are filled one at a time from right to left: ones digit, then the tens carry, then the tens digit, and so on. Carry cells are fillable circled digits, two sizes smaller than the working digits, stacked above the top factor. The first step’s carries sit immediately above the top number; each later step adds a new carry row above the previous one. Each partial row is the full product of the top number times that bottom digit — add the previous carry into the next place (`26 × 2` is `52`: write `2`, carry `1`, tens `5`; `17 × 9` is `153`: write `3`, carry `6`, tens `5`, hundreds `1`; `324 × 7` is `2268`). A leftover after the last top digit is the next result digit (`9 × 3` is `27`). `24 × 8` is `192`. Shifted partials skip the placeholder zeros: `9 × 1` is only `9` moved one column left, not `90`; `11 × 2` is `22` shifted, not `220`. A one-digit multiplier fills the product digits and carries only, including a leading leftover digit. A two-or-more-digit multiplier requires every partial and its carries, then the total digits and any summation carries.
- Division is shown as long division: the bracket, quotient, and subtract-and-bring-down working. Easy and Medium use a one-digit divisor (2–9). Hard is a 3–5 digit dividend ÷ 3–9. Challenge is a 4–7 digit dividend ÷ a two-digit divisor. Do not show a divisor times table, and do not show `a ÷ b` as a single inline equation only. Every required box is a single digit: each quotient digit, each multiply (divisor × quotient digit) digit, each subtract remainder digit, and each bring-down. The final quotient alone is not enough. Worksheet cells must show `0` (never treat it as empty), so `220 ÷ 20` stays `220`, not `22`. `220 ÷ 20` is `11`. Division stays exact; do not generate a remainder problem without a remainder field. A miss on a worksheet does not rewrite the problem as inline text (`Not quite. 220 ÷ 20 = 11`); reveal the filled sheet on a one-try miss, and in practice say try this step again.
- Addition and subtraction have one fillable line: the total. Pictures stay one blank. Addition and subtraction carries are shown only after reveal and are not filled.
- `worksheetFields(problem)` lists every slot the learner must fill. Multi-step multiplication and division are grouped by `worksheetSections`. The button is **Next** until the last section, then **Submit**. Next only advances when the current section is filled and correct. Division shows one quotient digit at a time, with that step’s multiply / subtract / bring-down. The keypad writes the active slot; tapping a slot selects it. Cell slots take one digit and then move to the next slot in the same section. The separate answer box stays hidden.
- Division must be exact. Subtraction must not produce a negative answer.
- `parseAnswer` accepts integers only, including a leading unicode minus (`−`).
- The play answer field is keypad-only: `readonly`, no system keyboard, and no typing into the field.
- Stay on the problem until the answer is correct, then advance. A miss scores 0 for that problem even if they later get it right (`creditsAnswer`). Do not jump to the next question on a miss. Results Correct is `formatCorrectCount(correct, answered)` — first-try correct over submitted answers (`10 / 11`).
- Quiz progress stars use `progressStars(correct, limit)`: one star animates in for each 20% of the quiz answered correctly, up to five.
- Unlimited practice and sprint use `unlimitedStars(attempts)`: one star for each all-correct set of 10 problems, counted from the first problem. A retried miss is not totally correct. After 7 stars, show `X ★` instead of more icons.
- Do not skip uniqueness forever; avoiding the immediate previous prompt is enough.
- After changing generation, run `node --experimental-default-type=module test-problems.mjs`.
